# UI Copy Rules

Generated UI copy should sound specific, direct, and useful. It should not read like generic AI marketing copy that could fit any product.

## Avoid

- "Here's the thing"
- "Unlock your potential"
- "Seamless experience"
- "Game-changing"
- "Revolutionary"
- "Transform the way you"
- "In today's fast-paced world"
- "At its core"
- "The future of"
- "Designed to empower"
- Vague claims like "The implications are significant"
- Throat-clearing openers
- Business jargon
- Meta-commentary
- Passive voice where a direct actor is better
- Dramatic formulaic contrasts
- Fake profundity
- Repeated three-item punchy lists
- Copy that could fit any product

## Prefer

- Specific product claims.
- Concrete user benefit.
- Short CTA labels.
- Human, plain language.
- Active voice.
- Product-specific headings.
- Body copy under 20 words in the hero where possible.
- One CTA label per intent.

## Examples

Bad: "Unlock seamless event memories with AI-powered sharing."

Better: "Collect every guest photo in one private gallery."

Bad: "Transform the way teams collaborate across devices."

Better: "Send text from your phone to your Mac in one tap."

Bad: "A revolutionary platform for modern creators."

Better: "Turn voice notes into project plans before the idea disappears."
